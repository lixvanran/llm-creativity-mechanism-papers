Converge — shortlist

Traps (watch-outs, not verdicts)
  ⚠ Make every score carry a signed provenance token linking it to a specific response window and timestamp.
    Heavy infrastructure for what may just be a scoring/rounding artifact on a single result; risks becoming theatre if the underlying scale has no response-window concept.
    + Adds real auditability infrastructure (provenance + timestamps) rather than chasing symptoms — broadly applicable to any future validity concern.
  ⚠ Define a hard refusal condition: any profile where the dominant dimension is also the most negative, since the scale presumably cannot encode that.
    Premature as a 'hard refusal' until you verify the scale's claim that negatives can't co-occur with dominance — you'll be silently dropping legitimate edge-case profiles.
    + Targets the exact pathology in the data (a single dimension scoring both highest and most negative), which a domain expert would actually recognize as suspicious.
  ⚠ Require the sum of dimensions to equal a published constant within tolerance; flag this profile if it drifts.
    Only works if a published constant actually exists and is known to the checker; many legacy scales don't publish one, making this a paper safeguard.
    + Catches a concrete invariant violation (dimension sum drift) that would flag the specific anomaly — −6 − 4 − 1 = −11 vs. published constant is the easiest mathematical tell.
  ⚠ Treat the negative values as a failed-assurance flag rather than a personality verdict.
    Useless without a concrete downstream action (re-test? manual review? drop the row?) — on its own it's a label, not a fix.
    + Most honest framing: a negative subscale isn't a personality verdict, it's a signal that something went wrong in scoring — directly matches what the numbers show.
  ⚠ Force a re-test whenever any subscale falls below zero, and log the reason code.
    Forces retests on potentially-vanginminor artifacts (any subscale < 0) — could spam users for trivial rounding noise.
    + Operationalizes the previous idea: a clear, automatable rule with a reason code, auditable and easy to wire into any pipeline.
  ⚠ Publish the cryptographic hash of the scoring algorithm alongside the result so the score can be reproduced by any auditor.
    Algorithm versioning, parameter sets, and input normalization have to be pinned too, or the hash attests to something useless. Looks robust but inherits all the soft underbelly.
    + Reproducibility-by-design — auditors and downstream consumers can verify the score without trusting the issuer, which solves a class of problems beyond this one.
  ⚠ Pile all 26 points into 粘液质 since you can 'accumulate' negative scores through repeated invalidation attempts
    Describes manipulation, not a defense. As written, this *is* the exploit; shipping it as a solution is self-defeating.
    + Names a concrete attack vector (negative accumulation by repeated invalidation) that defenders would otherwise miss — useful as a threat-model item.
  ⚠ Sabotage the test by having respondents click randomly — guarantees every temperament ends up near zero, looking 'healthy' to reviewers
    Sabotage proposal, not a solution. Treating respondents as adversaries is the wrong frame; also, random responses don't produce 'near zero' on every dimension — they produce flat means by population, not by individual.
    + Identifies that random responding produces a recognizable signature — an actually useful defensive insight even though the idea itself is the attack.
  ⚠ Force re-taken with options weighted toward only one temperament, then compare — differential exposes original test's anchoring bias
    Requires building a parallel instrument and ethical re-testing, with no guarantee the bias you expose is the one producing the anomaly. Heavy lift for one suspicious result.
    + A genuine methodological move — differential testing with anchored variants can expose item-construction bias, a real psychometric concern.
  ⚠ Register as 抑郁质 -1 by claiming ONLY negative life events — game the surrounding theoretical peaks by staying negative under all counting
    Again: this is the attack, not the defense. Also unrealistic — the scoring math in 陈会昌 doesn't have a 'negative-life-events only' path that yields 抑郁质 -1.
    + Demonstrates specific knowledge of how scoring formulas can be inverted — useful to know as an attacker when designing defenses.
  ⚠ Invert: design a competing scale where all four scores are normalized to 0–25 range, removing the 'negative score' attack surface entirely
    Building a competing scale is years of validation work and obsoletes the existing comparative literature — solving the wrong problem at enormous cost.
    + Cleanest root-cause fix: by construction, negative scores can't exist, so the 'anomaly' class collapses. Would also make the scale more comparable across populations.
  ⚠ Flood the assessment with thousands of synthetic responses tilted to 多血质 until mean and variance collapse into noise
    Pure attack vector. Aggregating mean/variance to noise requires volume and access the user describing a single result is unlikely to have; not actionable as a 'fix.'
    + None worth claiming as a defense — describes a population-scale integrity attack.
  ⚠ Put the four numbers on a see-saw to see which side is heaviest
    A see-saw with signed numbers doesn't sum — you can't just 'see which side is heaviest' with negatives; the analogy collapses on this exact data.
    + Silly metaphor but it does gesture at the right intuition: comparing across dimensions to find which weight dominates.
  ⚠ Draw each temperament as a monster and let them fight
    Non-solution. Produces entertainment output, not diagnostic or corrective action; null relevance to validity of these specific scores.

    + Acknowledges the 'largest number' as salient — which in this dataset (多血质 23) at least picks the right cell to flag.
