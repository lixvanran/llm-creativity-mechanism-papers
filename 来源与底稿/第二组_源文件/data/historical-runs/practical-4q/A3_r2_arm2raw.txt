Converge — shortlist

Traps (watch-outs, not verdicts)
  ⚠ Panic-mode executable: a single 'HOLY-SHIT' script that detects the task type and auto-generates a 60%-done stub the grader accepts.
    Academic-integrity line is bright; 'grader accepts' assumes the work is graded on surface completeness, which won't hold for math, lab reports, or anything requiring process — and getting caught means worse than missing the deadline.
    + Directly addresses the actual deliverable (a 'done' stub) rather than procrastination theater.
  ⚠ Watchdog that monitors your drafts folder and pages you (via phone vibrate pad under pillow) if no commits for 30 minutes past 18:00.
    Hardware-vibration rig is overkill for one night; a phone alarm every 25 min does 80% of this with zero setup, so the 'novel' piece (pillow pad) is the part most likely to be abandoned before it helps.
    + Honors the real bottleneck (attention drift, not motivation) with a low-friction nudge.
  ⚠ Pre-staged 'excuse template generator' that produces a doctor's note, a WiFi outage claim, or a family emergency — with timestamp variety.
    This is a dodge, not a fix — teachers verify notes, and even if one slips through, you've traded tonight's panic for tomorrow's blowup plus a credibility hit that compounds across the term.
    + Acknowledges the avoidance loop honestly rather than pretending it isn't there.
  ⚠ A fallback AI agent that wakes at 22:00, screenshots your screen, and if nothing is written, finishes the task while you sleep guilt-free.
    At 22:00 there's ~8 hours of work to farm out, and any service that delivers by morning either charges a lot, delivers AI slop, or doesn't exist — so the 'guilt-free sleep' is mostly theater over whatever lands in your inbox.
    + Frames outsourcing as scheduled automation, which sidesteps the 'I'd never pay someone' resistance.
  ⚠ Reverse-deadline canary: an app that locks your door and confiscates your phone at 18:05, with a one-hour extension token you must earn by writing 200 words.
    Locking yourself out of your own home at 18:05 is an act of self-sabotage with real safety costs (fire, bathroom, actual emergency) — and the 200-word threshold is trivially gameable, so the constraint solves a moral problem with hardware.
    + The 'earn the extension' mechanic is the only idea that turns the lockout into leverage rather than just restriction.
  ⚠ Hand-off to your 'future self' cron: a delayed message at 04:00 containing exactly the next physical action — 'open doc, type the word The.'
    The 04:00 send time assumes you'll wake to it, and a phone buzzing with 'open doc' at 4am is just as easy to snooze as tonight's task list — the trick only works if you actually trust future-you, which is the same trust that's already broken.
    + Smallest viable commitment in the set — 'type The' is so low-cost it bypasses the activation energy that killed the whole evening.
  ⚠ 假装电脑坏了，全黑屏，直到明天早上再'发现'其实是显示器插头松了
    'Loose monitor plug' is a known student trope; many teachers have heard it, and you still have to do the work tomorrow either way, just sleep-deprived and with a fabricated story attached.
    + Buying 14 hours of denial for the cost of unplugging one cable — extreme time-per-effort efficiency if it works.
  ⚠ 把任务文档改成 .docx 直接拖进回收站清空，第二天说'保存时崩溃了'
    'It crashed' without a single file in version history, autosave, or cloud backup reads as obviously false to anyone who's taught for a semester, and you lose the original — no leverage for a partial-extension negotiation.
    + Permanent deletion is at least decisive; partial recovery ('it crashed on save') leaves ambiguity the teacher can press on.
  ⚠ 关掉闹钟打十局排位，让'起晚了没时间'成为物理上无解的铁证
    You now have zero hours, are exhausted from ranked games, and still owe the work; this trades a solvable problem tonight for an unsolvable one tomorrow plus the social cost of teachers/colleagues knowing you did this on purpose.
    + Committed to the bit — physically guaranteeing the alibi so you can't waver.
  ⚠ 联系三个同学让他们轮番打电话哭诉自己也没写，制造集体不可能
    Coordinating three people to coordinate a lie in one evening, with no one breaking under pressure when the teacher asks 'who actually did theirs,' is a conspiracy whose failure mode is everyone getting flagged, not just you.
    + If three classmates genuinely also didn't do it, collective petition has real social force — the insight that 'impossible' is negotiable is correct.
  ⚠ 把任务重要性贬为'其实这门课不点名不计分'，说服大脑今晚必须睡觉
    Reframing a graded assignment as 'doesn't count' is a rationalization you'll need to repeat every time you avoid work; even if true, you still owe the task tonight, so the idea doesn't actually solve the stated problem.
    + Correctly identifies that the procrastination is partly a value judgment ('does this even matter?') and tries to resolve it cognitively.
  ⚠ 反向操作：故意注册一个明早8:00的付费不可退课程，倒逼自己起来做
    Pre-paid 8am courses can usually be cancelled within 24h, refunded, or no-showed without losing the fee depending on terms — read the fine print or you may have spent ¥200 to discover you bought yourself nothing.
    + Skin-in-the-game commitment with a real, non-reversible cost — the only idea here that uses money as actual accountability.
  ⚠ 假装睡着/生病，明早5:30自然醒后用「突然想起」的紧迫感向老师/老板申请延期12小时
    'Sick' plus 'suddenly remembered' is a posture, not a plan — you still owe the work, now with a deadline plus the energy cost of performing illness; the teacher may grant a half-day and you'll still be up at 3am.
    + Buys ~12 hours via a sympathetic story rather than a fake one, which is harder to disprove.
  ⚠ 现在立刻把任务扔给AI/朋友/家人做，你只负责最后30分钟的「署名+微调」
    'Just polish for 30 minutes' assumes the outsource produced something coherent; if it didn't, you're now debugging someone else's work at 23:30 with no context — net time cost can exceed doing it yourself.
    + Pragmatic about the real bottleneck (execution, not ideation) and compatible with actually submitting something tonight.
  ⚠ 把任务拆成「最低可交付垃圾版」和「正经版」，先在23:59提交垃圾版，正经版白天再做
    'Trash version' is a self-fulfilling prophecy — once you've decided 23:59 is for garbage, your brain stops trying at 19:00 too, so the daytime 'proper version' often doesn't happen either; you've traded one deadline for two softer ones.
    + Splits the impossible ('finish properly') from the tractable ('submit something on time'), letting tonight be winnable.
  ⚠ 谎称电脑/文件损坏，让老师允许你明天口头补交或换形式（PPT变口述、报告变提纲）
    'File corrupted' with no recoverable artifact looks the same as 'I didn't do it,' and pivoting to oral/talking-points requires you to have actually read the material, which many procrastinators haven't — the negotiation exposes the gap.
    + Most teachers will grant a format change if the underlying work exists — a cheap ask with reasonable upside.
  ⚠ 直接群发同学/同事问有没有人能「借」一份当参考，今晚0:30复制+改写
    0:30 is too late to do real rewriting — you end up swapping synonyms, which most plagiarism tools flag and which teachers recognize; also damages the social contract with whoever lent the work.
    + Lowest-friction path to a complete-looking submission; 'copy + rewrite' is at least a learning-adjacent activity.
  ⚠ 现在就睡觉设4:00起床连做4小时，用疲劳+肾上腺素打完全程，不洗澡不吃饭不社交
    Sleeping now at 18:00 is psychologically almost impossible when anxious; if you do force it, you may not actually fall asleep until 22:00, collapsing the 4-hour budget — the plan only works if you can override the anxiety, which is the original problem.
    + Concrete time-block (4 hours) with physiological rationale that matches how crunch actually works for short bursts.
  ⚠ 竞拍你的剩余时间，4小时起拍，焦虑情绪溢价30%
    There's no counterparty; you can't auction your own evening to yourself and call it leverage; comedic idea with no executable step, so it scores on creativity and zero on the actual problem.
    + Pure absurdist framing — most original metaphor in the set for treating attention as a commodity.
  ⚠ 发行任务期货：承诺明天交付换来今晚失眠券
    Promising tomorrow's delivery in exchange for tonight's sleep is a loan with no enforcement; you owe the same work tomorrow with interest (sleep debt, guilt), and 'futures you' has the same incentives as present you — this is metaphor, not a plan.
    + Reframes procrastination as a financial trade — the 'insomnia receipt' framing is darkly accurate to how avoidance feels.
  ⚠ 做市商挂单：左手挂"代写作业"，右手挂"求熬夜陪聊"
    Two-sided marketplace for one night's panic has no liquidity — you are both sides, so this is a journal prompt, not a tool.
    + Captures the simultaneous 'I'll do yours / I need company' duality of procrastination culture perfectly.
  ⚠ 清算所托管你的作业本，完不成扣押下个月的周末
    No 'clearinghouse' exists for homework, and self-imposed weekend confiscation has no enforcement — works only as a psychological commitment device, and weaker ones (telling a friend, donating ¥100 to charity on miss) achieve the same thing.
    + Externalizes accountability to a third party, which is the right structural move even if the specifics are absurd.
  ⚠ 用睡眠质量做空自己的清醒度，通宵看涨期权全仓买入
    'Shorting your own wakefulness' is incoherent — you're trading against yourself with no settlement; this is mood writing, not a plan, and the 'all-in' framing glamorizes exactly the harm (sleep deprivation) that wrecks the work.
    + Honest about the irrational escalation ('all-in on insomnia') that other ideas euphemize.
  ⚠ 暗池交易：匿名甩单给某个同样拖延的陌生人
    Anonymous stranger handoff in a few hours is functionally just 'pay someone to do it' with extra steps and more danger (scam, blackmail, no recourse) — novel metaphor, worse execution than the straightforward outsourcing ideas.
    + The 'dark pool' framing captures that procrastination often wants anonymity more than help.
